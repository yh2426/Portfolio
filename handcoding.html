Interactive Hand Heart Game

An interactive experience built with p5.js and ml5.js HandPose exploring physical connection.

01. Concept & Reflection

The main idea of this project was to explore how physical distance can affect our emotions and how we connect with others. At first, I considered BodyPose for tracking, but I found that positioning two people for a single camera was difficult, especially when sharing one screen.

I pivoted to HandPose, which proved more responsive and easier to use when players are close together in front of one webcam.

02. Interaction Instructions

Step 1: Start

Raise one hand and point your index finger toward a gray heart on the screen to begin.

Step 2: Draw

A heart appears between your thumb and index finger; move your hand to draw.

Step 3: Customize

Pinch your thumb and index finger together to change the heart's color.

Step 4: Connect

Bring your hands closer to the other player. A big red heart will appear when you get close enough.

03. Future Roadmap

In the future, I hope to continue working on this project by making it work over a network using WebSockets. This would allow people to connect and interact from different locations using their own cameras.
