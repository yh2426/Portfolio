About Me.

I am an XR Designer currently pursuing my Master’s at NYU Integrated Design & Media (IDM). My practice focuses on creating immersive experiences by integrating human-centered design principles with creative coding and technical development.

With a deep interest in Creative Coding, I leverage code as a tool to create dynamic interactions and meaningful spatial narratives. My academic journey at the University of Toronto gives me a unique perspective on cognitive load and user perception, while my background from the University of Waterloo provided me with a rigorous foundation in the intersection of UX design, creative coding, and business.

I specialize in creating environments that are both visually compelling and intuitively functional. From generative art with p5.js to high-fidelity experiences in Unity and Unreal Engine 5, I am dedicated to designing the next generation of immersive media.
