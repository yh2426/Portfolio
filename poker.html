Poker Memory Game

Cognitive Micro-break: Enhancing Focus Through Play.

01. UX Research & Personas

My research focused on three core employee groups: the Efficiency Seeker, the Overwhelmed Employee, and the Growth-Minded Individual. The goal was to understand how a simple game could alleviate work stress and provide a brief cognitive "reset" during intense working hours.

02. Interaction Design & Flow

I mapped out the Game Flow to ensure the transitions between the Start Menu, Gameplay, and End results were intuitive and minimized cognitive load.

03. Technical Development

Built using Processing (Java), the project involved complex state management and array logic to handle card flipping and match detection.
