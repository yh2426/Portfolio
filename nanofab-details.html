Nanofab VR Training Modules

360-degree VR training modules built in Uptale — NYU Tandon Digital Learning, Nanofab VR initiative.

01. Project Overview

As a Graduate Production Assistant with NYU Tandon's Digital Learning group, I built 360-degree VR training modules in Uptale for the Nanofab VR initiative — designing the scenes, hotspots, overlays, and navigation flows that make up each module. I also designed the project's companion website.

02. QA & Platform Testing

I also ran QA testing across headset and web builds, checking each module for navigation, interaction, and audio issues before it went out on the platform.
