Revela: AI-Powered Personalized Skincare

[The Problem Space]

Skincare businesses in Africa rely heavily on guesswork and generalized solutions to recommend products, leading to customer frustration, inconsistent product results, and damaged brand reputation. Research shows that there are only 2, 288 Certified Dermatologists in Africa. About 95% of skincare products are not made for the black skin.

[Focus on personalized skincare routines]

Our platform leverages AI-powered facial scanning and user-provided responses to deliver unique, personalized skincare routine recommendations. By analyzing individual skin conditions and preferences, we ensure tailored solutions that address each user's specific needs for healthier, radiant skin.

[Focus on Technology]

Our platform leverages advanced AI analysis to transform the skincare experience. By utilizing cutting-edge image recognition technology, the AI accurately identifies skin conditions within seconds and delivers tailored recommendations matched to individual needs. This innovation not only ensures precise, science-backed solutions but also empowers users with a personalized and efficient approach to achieving healthier, radiant skin.

[Our Solution]

Revela eliminates this guesswork. Our AI-powered platform analyzes customers’ skin through advanced image recognition technology, then matches their skin’s specific needs to the active ingredients in a business’s product portfolio. This provides highly accurate and personalized product recommendations, leading to better results for customers and a competitive edge for businesses.
