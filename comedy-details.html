Unreal Comedic Short Film

A solo virtual production project exploring absurd dialogue and digital puppetry.

01. The Story

The story takes place in an abandoned military base. Two soldiers are assigned to guard the site, even though it appears completely deserted. They don’t know why they are there, which creates a quiet, absurd, and funny situation for their conversation.

02. MetaHuman & 3D Scanning

To make the characters look realistic, I started with a 3D Reality Scan of real actors. Then, I imported and synthesized the data into MetaHuman framework to build the final 3D digital characters.

03. Motion Capture & Live Link

Comedic timing depends entirely on expressions. I combined body motion capture with Unreal Engine's Live Link Face to track the actors' faces in real-time, capturing every subtle micro-expression for the deadpan dialogue.
